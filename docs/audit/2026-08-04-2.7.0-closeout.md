## 2. What the repair work uncovered that no audit had

Four defects were found by checking that a repair preserved behaviour — an oracle, not a review:

1. **`kdtree_within_radius` wrong on 274/400 queries.** `split_val` came from `items[split]` while
   the partition was made at `mid_val`; **3,412** points sat on the wrong side of an ancestor's
   plane. Shipped three releases. The 2.6.14 kd-tree assertions pass on such a tree.
2. **`solve_gmres` overshot its matvec budget without bound.**
3. **`triangulate_polygon` documented a return length it does not always deliver.**
4. **Two findings had never been scheduled at all** — found only by assigning a disposition to
   every numbered finding instead of reasoning from tier summaries.

## 3. Three corrections to my own analysis, in the order they were made

Recorded because the method failure is the transferable part:

- **The duplicate-globals finding was dismissed as overstated** on a `grep` whose pattern assumed a
  single space before `=`. All four globals really were duplicated. *A gate found what the grep
  missed.*
- **The incircle finding was filed red** on the claim that `delaunay_2d`'s super-triangle sits far
  outside the data. It sits at **10x** the extent; with the real geometry, 0/180 disagree.
  *The probe generated coordinates by magnitude class instead of reconstructing the caller's
  geometry.*
- **The private-symbol coupling was reported as five pairs including a cycle.** It is six pairs and
  acyclic; the extra edges were comment-only mentions. *Comments are not code.*

## 4. Standing conclusions

- **Coverage is the root cause, again.** Every defect in §2 lived in a function with no assertion
  on its behaviour. `spatial.cyr` produced two of them and stood at 3/14 functions covered.
- **An assertion that cannot fail is worse than none** — it reports safety. Six such classes were
  repaired; adversarial review then found ~35 mutants the *repairs* still missed, and those were
  closed too.
- **Gates beat greps.** Every ad-hoc scan in this release that contradicted a finding was wrong;
  every mechanical gate was right.
- **A faster wrong answer is worse than a slow right one.** Three confirmed optimisations were
  withheld on that basis.
