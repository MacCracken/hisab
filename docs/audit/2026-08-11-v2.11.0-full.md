**Method**: mechanical class sweeps first, then six parallel dimensions, every finding handed to an
independent skeptic instructed to **refute** it

## The headline: one root cause, ~24 sites

**24 of the 52 findings are the same defect.** A guard compares a quantity against a threshold that
is wrong for that quantity — either a **squared** value against the unsquared `EPSILON_F64`, or an
**absolute** epsilon against a quantity that scales with the problem. In every case the function
then **fabricates a plausible answer** rather than reporting.

does not fix the code, and does not reach the other thirty-four modules.

## Third: the suite itself

The test dimension audited the 3507 assertions rather than the source, and its two criticals are
about what the suite cannot see:

* **38.4% of value-changing single-operator mutants survive all five suites.**
* **836 of 3510 assertions (23.8%) compare through `f64_to`, which TRUNCATES** — so any error below
  1.0 is invisible to nearly a quarter of the suite.
* 29 public functions are covered only by `assert_neq(f(...), 0)` — a pointer check, not a value check.
* `m3_normal_matrix` has zero value coverage; 8 of 9 spherical-harmonic bands are never asserted.

That explains the whole audit: **every finding above was invisible to 3507 assertions and 99%
coverage**, and now there is a measured reason why.

## Plan for the rest

**One release, organised around one rule**, not twenty scattered patches:

> **Guard exactly what makes the operation fail, and nothing more.** For a division that is
> `DBL_MIN`. A threshold that needs a scale chosen for it is the defect.

1. **The epsilon tier** (~20 sites). Sweep by mechanical grep, not review. Each site: does the
   guarded quantity scale with the problem? If yes the threshold must too, or be `DBL_MIN`. The
   permanent assertion is **scale covariance** — the same problem in different units — because that
   is the only shape of test a bad threshold cannot pass, and it must **bracket every threshold a
   repair might plausibly have chosen**, not just the chosen one.
2. **The allocation tier** (3 remaining). Mechanical, low risk, identical repair.
3. **The abort tier.** `halfedge_from_triangles` and three `collision_core` entry points abort the
   process. A library must not.
4. **The suite tier.** The `f64_to` truncation is the highest-value item in the whole audit: fixing
   it strengthens 836 existing assertions at once.
5. **Re-verify the 28** before any of them is touched.

⚠ **Do not batch tier 1 into one commit.** Each site is a behaviour change on a documented entry
point; the tree's own history says these land in mutation-proven bites.

## What this audit did NOT reach

`transforms`, `noise_simplex`, `einsum`, `tensor` beyond bounds-checking, `ode` beyond one stiff

correctness-shaped; nothing audited **performance regressions** or the **benchmark harness**.
