## AU1 — the 2026-08-11 "did NOT reach" list

**Coverage.**
- **SIMD**: the x86 and aarch64 `f64v_*` emitters read at the cyrius 6.7.9 tag; a differential harness ran 6,000
  records in six modes (uniform, random exponent over binary64, special values, sign-heavy zeros, ±2^1000…2^1023,
  the subnormal band) through all 16 public functions that use `f64v_*` (96,000 calls), bit for bit against the
  contract's scalar form in IEEE doubles. Bit-identical: `hvec2_lerp`, `hvec3_lerp`, `hvec3_add/_sub/_mul/_scale`,
  `hvec4_add/_sub/_scale`, `m3_mul`, `m3_mul_vec3`, `m4_mul`, `m4_mul_vec4`. `hvec4_dot`/`hquat_dot` sum as
  (x·x′ + z·z′) + (y·y′ + w·w′), 405/6,000 records ≠ a left-to-right sum (mostly 1 ulp); no contract states an
  order, and both orders have the same worst-case error against the exact sum: not a defect.

  dynamically or by read/write order for the alias a caller would write. Clean dynamically: `eigen_symmetric`,

  53 hisab, 8 stdlib) and classified 764 call sites in `src/`, `tests/`, `examples/` (728 pair-binds, 21 `?`,
  15 tail returns): **0 offences**. Positive control: a planted file seen on every line.
- **Cross-module conventions**: 200 random axis/angle pairs agree with a numpy Rodrigues rotation within 9.2e-16
  across `hquat_rotate_vec3`, `m3_from_quat`→`m3_mul_vec3`, `m4_from_srt`→`m4_transform_point`, `t3d_apply`, the

### SIMD

- **F-S1** · low · `hvec3_dot`, `hvec4_dot`, `hquat_dot` — an all-negative-zero sum comes back +0.
  `hvec3_dot(hvec3_new(0,0,0), hvec3_new(BFF0000000000000 ×3))` → 0x0000000000000000; IEEE oracle
  ((0·−1) + (0·−1)) + (0·−1) = 0x8000000000000000. 206/6,000 records (vec3), 101/6,000 (vec4, quat). Cause: x86
  `EMIT_F64V_DOT` starts its accumulator at `xorpd` +0. The two divides by a dot (`geo_diff.cyr` :445, :487 in
  3.3.7) refuse a non-finite reciprocal. Lane only (= AU2-G7-F10). **[3.4.1]** — the cause is cyrius's emitter: a
  hisab-side form or an upstream report (maintainer's approval) is the choice.
- **F-S2** · low · `hvec4_dot`, `hquat_dot` and `hquat_length`/`_normalize`/`_inverse`/`_slerp` through
  `hquat_length_sq` — not bit-identical across targets: aarch64 6.7.9 accumulates with `fmla v2.2d`, so for n = 4
  the second product per lane is fused (fma(a2, b2, a0·b0)) where x86 rounds twice. `hvec3_dot` is the same on both.

  **[3.4.1]** (extend the note).
